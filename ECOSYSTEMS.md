# OpenGrant OS Ecosystem Strategy

## Overview

OpenGrant OS is designed as an open infrastructure layer that can support multiple Web3 ecosystems.

The platform aims to improve transparency and efficiency in decentralized grant management.

---

## Target Ecosystems

## Ethereum Ecosystem

Objectives:

- Support open-source Web3 projects
- Improve grant coordination
- Enable transparent funding workflows

Potential integrations:

- Ethereum-based grant programs
- DAOs
- Public goods funding initiatives

---

## Solana Ecosystem

Objectives:

- Support Solana builders
- Connect developers with ecosystem opportunities
- Improve grant discovery

Potential integrations:

- Solana developer communities
- Ecosystem funding programs

---

## Polygon Ecosystem

Objectives:

- Support scalable Web3 applications
- Enable community funding programs

Potential integrations:

- Polygon builders
- Web3 impact projects

---

## Base Ecosystem

Objectives:

- Support new Web3 startups
- Improve access to ecosystem funding

Potential integrations:

- Builder communities
- Startup programs

---

## Future Expansion

Future versions may support:

- Multi-chain grant discovery
- Cross-chain identity
- Blockchain-based reputation
- DAO governance systems

---

## Partnership Vision

OpenGrant OS aims to collaborate with blockchain foundations, DAOs, communities and developers to build a more transparent funding ecosystem.
