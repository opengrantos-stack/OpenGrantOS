# OpenGrant OS Pitch

## Building the Future of Web3 Grant Infrastructure

---

## The Opportunity

The Web3 ecosystem is growing rapidly, but grant funding remains fragmented and inefficient.

Thousands of builders struggle to find funding opportunities and navigate complex application processes.

---

## The Solution

OpenGrant OS is an open-source infrastructure platform that connects:

- Grant providers
- Builders
- Reviewers
- Communities

through a transparent grant management system.

---

## Why Now?

Web3 innovation needs better funding infrastructure.

A transparent and efficient grant system can accelerate:

- Open-source development
- Blockchain innovation
- Community-driven projects

---

## Core Product

OpenGrant OS provides:

- Grant discovery
- Application management
- Reviewer workflows
- Milestone tracking
- Transparent funding records

---

## Target Users

### Builders

Find opportunities and manage applications.

### Foundations

Manage grant programs efficiently.

### Communities

Increase transparency and participation.

---

## Vision

Become the open-source infrastructure layer powering the next generation of Web3 funding.

---

## Current Stage

Prototype preparation phase.

Building documentation, architecture and MVP strategy.
